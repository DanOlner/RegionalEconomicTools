# ONS Regional GVA by Industry — why isn't the next release out?

*Briefing compiled 2 June 2026. Sources are linked inline and listed at the foot.*

## The short version

The annual **"Regional gross value added (balanced) by industry: all ITL regions"** dataset — the one that used to land reliably each April — was last published on **17 April 2025** (reference data to 2023). Its release page now lists the next release simply as **"To be announced."** There is no scheduled date.

No single ONS notice says "we are delaying regional GVA by industry because X." The explanation has to be assembled from two things that are both clearly on the record:

1. The release page itself confirms there is **no scheduled next release** ("To be announced"), and
2. ONS is in the middle of a major restructure in which **subnational statistics have been explicitly flagged for "reconsideration"** and specific subnational/regional outputs have already been cut.

So the most accurate framing is: the release hasn't been cancelled or formally announced as delayed — it has been left **un-scheduled** while ONS reprioritises around core economic statistics, and the subnational portfolio (which this product sits inside) is one of the areas under active review and reduction.

## What we can confirm about the dataset

- **Product:** Regional gross value added (balanced) by industry: all ITL regions — current price estimates, chained volume measures and implied deflators for UK countries and ITL1/ITL2/ITL3 regions, with detailed industry breakdown.
- **Last release:** 17 April 2025.
- **Next release:** "To be announced" (i.e. not in the release calendar).
- A correction notice dated **10 October 2025** appears on the dataset page (relating to earlier-year data), but that is a revision to already-published figures, not a new annual edition.
- The closely related, older **"GVA by industry by local authority"** dataset is even more stale — last released **11 January 2021** (data only to 2018), also "TBA" — showing this family of subnational industry breakdowns has had timeliness problems for a while, predating the current restructure.

## Why it's not out: the restructure context

The ONS has spent 2025–26 in a publicly acknowledged crisis over the quality of its core economic statistics, and the institutional response has squeezed exactly the kind of "second-tier" subnational output that regional GVA by industry represents.

**The Devereux Review.** An independent review of ONS performance, culture, leadership and structure, led by Sir Robert Devereux, was launched on **1 April 2025** and reported in **June 2025**. It assessed how effectively ONS delivers its core statistical outputs and major programmes. ([ONS statement on the Devereux Review](https://www.ons.gov.uk/news/statementsandletters/onsstatementinresponsetothedevereuxreview))

**The Office for Statistics Regulation (OSR) systemic review.** Running in parallel, OSR conducted and published a systemic review of the economic statistics produced by ONS, reinforcing the "quality first" message. ([OSR final report](https://osr.statisticsauthority.gov.uk/news/office-for-statistics-regulation-publishes-final-report-on-ons-economic-statistics/))

**"Restoring Confidence, Improving Quality" (26 June 2025).** ONS set out its plan for economic statistics and surveys. The headline is a deliberate shift to **"quality over quantity"**: focus resources on the statistics underpinning the most critical economic and societal decisions — **GDP, prices, labour market and population** — and accept **a reduction in statistical outputs** in the near term. The plan commits to **cutting statistical outputs by around 10% in 2026**. Crucially for this topic, it states that ONS is *"stepping back from health statistics, **reconsidering the subnational statistics portfolio**, and prioritising the Living Costs and Food Survey over the Annual Population Survey."* ([ONS news](https://www.ons.gov.uk/news/news/restoringconfidenceimprovingqualityourplansforeconomicstatisticsandonssurveys) · [ONS blog](https://blog.ons.gov.uk/2025/06/26/restoring-confidence-improving-quality-our-plans-for-economic-statistics-and-ons-surveys/))

That phrase — "reconsidering the subnational statistics portfolio" — is the closest thing to an official explanation. Regional GVA by industry is part of that subnational portfolio, and "reconsidering" plausibly explains why the annual edition has not been re-scheduled.

**Latest steps (23 February 2026).** ONS confirmed further reductions to deliver the 10% cut, and several land directly on subnational/regional economic outputs:
- *"Stopping further development of some subnational statistics — Health, Wellbeing and Place research will not progress... **Subnational estimates of tourism spending and Tourism Direct Gross Value Added will be stopped.**"*
- Plus: quarterly greenhouse-gas emissions paused, the wellbeing dashboard cut from quarterly to annual, night-time economy and lifestyle/risk-factor analysis ended, and Annual Population Survey resource reduced.

This shows subnational GVA-adjacent products (Tourism Direct GVA at subnational level) being actively withdrawn — consistent with the broader regional GVA-by-industry series slipping off the calendar. ([ONS: "Latest steps reaffirm commitment to quality over quantity"](https://www.ons.gov.uk/news/news/onslateststepsreaffirmcommitmenttoqualityoverquantity))

**April 2026 progress update.** ONS's quarterly progress update on the improvement plan still refers only briefly to "reconsidering the subnational statistics portfolio" and gives **no specific date or plan** for regional GVA by industry — i.e. as of the latest update there is still no published timetable for the next edition. ([Quarterly progress update, April 2026](https://www.ons.gov.uk/aboutus/ourstrategyandplans/onsimprovementplans/onseconomicstatisticsandsurveysimprovementplan/onseconomicstatisticsandsurveysimprovementplanquarterlyprogressupdateapril2026))

## Other contributing factors worth noting

These predate the restructure but compound the timeliness problem and are documented by ONS:

- **Post-pandemic / post-Census data dependencies.** ONS has noted delays to regional accounts linked to the COVID-19 period, and that subnational estimates depend on population data from Census 2021 (with Scotland's census, and therefore some ITL2/ITL3 Scottish breakdowns, subject to additional delay). ([Subnational economic activity statistics: user guide](https://www.ons.gov.uk/economy/grossdomesticproductgdp/methodologies/subnationaleconomicactivitystatisticsuserguide))
- **Recent data-quality corrections** in the regional GVA deflation/chain-linking process at ITL3 and local-authority level, which is exactly the kind of methods work that gets prioritised over publishing a new edition when resources are tight.

## Bottom line for your purposes

- **It is not formally cancelled**, but there is **no scheduled date** for the next annual regional GVA by industry release — the calendar entry is "To be announced."
- The **proximate cause is the ONS restructure**: a deliberate, publicly stated reprioritisation toward core economic statistics (GDP, prices, labour market, population) and a ~10% cut in total outputs in 2026, with the **subnational statistics portfolio explicitly named as under reconsideration** and some subnational GVA-adjacent products already stopped.
- If you need the latest position, the authoritative place to watch is the dataset's own page (the "Next release" field) and the ONS economic-statistics improvement-plan progress updates, which are where any new date would first appear.
