Author

Notes on the data

This is:

  • BRES full time job count data at local authority level for 2015 to 2024
  • 2 digit SIC sectors are used (shortened names)
  • Keeping only sectors with more than 1% of each local authority’s total job count
  • The 2022-24 average jobcount is included as job data can be volatile; ‘jobs2024’ is the latest jobcount datapoint in BRES.
  • Tables are ordered by location quotient (LQ) to show which sectors are more concentrated in each local authority. E.g. Sheffield’s top LQ sector is basic metals: its LQ of 5.13 = basic metals job count there is 5.1x higher than the Great Britain average. (BRES data is GB not UK.)
  • Compound Annual Growth Rate figures (just the year on year percent change taken from line-slopes through the data). Two are included: for the full 2015-2024 range, and for 2021-2024. (These use all the data, not smoothed).
